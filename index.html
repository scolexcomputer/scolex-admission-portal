Welcome to Scolex Computer Classes

Welcome to Scolex Computer Classes, one of the leading computer training institutes in Motipur, Bihar. Established in 2016 by Sarfaraz Faijee, our institute has been dedicated to providing quality computer education with practical, career-oriented training. Over the years, we have successfully trained 1000+ students, helping them build strong digital skills and achieve success in academics, competitive examinations, and professional careers.

At Scolex Computer Classes, we offer a wide range of professional courses including DCA, ADCA, DCA with Tally, DTP, Tally Prime with GST, Programming Languages (C, C++, Java, Python), Web Designing, HTML, CSS, and many more. Our experienced faculty, modern computer lab, smart classrooms, practical learning approach, and affordable fee structure ensure that every student receives the best learning experience.

Our mission is to empower students with the latest computer knowledge, improve their confidence, and prepare them for higher education, government examinations, private sector jobs, freelancing, and entrepreneurship. At Scolex Computer Classes, we believe that quality education and practical skills are the foundation of a successful future.
