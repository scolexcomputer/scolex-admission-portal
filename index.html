About Us

"Scolex Computer Classes is a trusted computer training institute located in Motipur, Bihar, established in 2016 by Sarfaraz Faijee with the vision of making quality computer education accessible and affordable for every student."

For nearly a decade, we have focused on providing practical, skill-based training that prepares students for real-world challenges. Our courses are designed to strengthen technical knowledge while building confidence and professional skills.

With experienced instructors, well-equipped computer labs, smart classrooms, and a student-friendly learning environment, we ensure every learner receives personalized guidance and hands-on experience. Our curriculum is regularly updated to match current industry requirements, enabling students to stay ahead in today's digital world.

Our mission is to empower students with modern computer knowledge and practical skills that open doors to higher education, government examinations, private-sector employment, freelancing, and entrepreneurship.

At Scolex Computer Classes, we believe that quality education, practical learning, and continuous innovation are the keys to a brighter future. We remain committed to helping every student achieve their academic and career goals through excellence in computer education.
